# Outreach System

## Goal

Start conversations with real prospects, learn how they currently find funding, and convert interested people into:
- lead magnet subscribers
- beta buyers
- discovery calls

## Priority Audiences

Start with these three:
- nonprofit executive directors
- development and fundraising managers
- independent grant writers or grant consultants

## Where to Find Them

- LinkedIn
- nonprofit association directories
- local charity and community organization listings
- grant-writing groups
- nonprofit Facebook groups
- sector newsletters and event attendee lists

## Founder Positioning

Use this frame consistently:

I’m building a funding intelligence product for Canadian nonprofits and grant-focused teams. It helps surface relevant opportunities faster, with deadlines, fit notes, and a cleaner tracking workflow.

## Cold Outreach Principles

- Keep the first message short
- Lead with relevance, not a pitch
- Ask for a small next step
- Use their language back to them in follow-ups
- Track every reply and objection

## LinkedIn Message 1

Hi [First Name] — I’m building a funding intelligence product for Canadian nonprofits and grant-focused teams. It’s designed to make grant discovery less scattered by organizing opportunities, deadlines, and fit notes in one place. I’m speaking with a few people in the space to make sure I build the right thing. Would you be open to a quick 15-minute chat?

## LinkedIn Message 2

Hi [First Name] — I came across your work and thought you might have useful perspective. I’m working on a tool for Canadian nonprofits that makes funding research and deadline tracking more structured and less time-consuming. I’m looking for a few early conversations with people who deal with grants in the real world. Open to a brief chat this week?

## Email Outreach Version

Subject:
Quick question about how you track funding opportunities

Body:

Hi [First Name],

I’m building a funding intelligence product for Canadian nonprofits and grant-focused organizations. The goal is to make funding discovery and deadline tracking more organized, so teams spend less time searching across scattered portals, newsletters, and saved links.

I’m speaking with a small number of people who already work close to this problem, and your perspective would be genuinely useful.

Would you be open to a short 15-minute conversation this week?

Best,
[Your Name]

## Soft Lead Magnet Outreach

Hi [First Name] — I put together a practical guide called `25 Funding Sources for Canadian Nonprofits` for teams that want a cleaner starting point for funding research. If it would be useful, I’m happy to send it over.

## Beta Offer Follow-Up

Hi [First Name] — thanks again for the conversation. Based on what I’m hearing, I’m putting together an early version of the `Funding Intelligence Vault` with curated opportunities, deadline tracking structure, and fit notes for Canadian nonprofits. I’m opening a small beta at founding pricing for early users. Want me to send the details?

## No-Pressure Follow-Up

Hi [First Name] — just following up in case this is relevant. I’m still speaking with nonprofit and grant-focused teams about how they currently find and track opportunities. If now isn’t the right time, no problem at all. If it is, I’d still value a short chat.

## Follow-Up Cadence

Use this rhythm:
- Day 1: first message
- Day 3: short follow-up
- Day 7: send lead magnet offer
- Day 12: beta mention
- Day 18: final soft close

## CRM Fields

Track each contact with:
- name
- role
- organization
- niche
- platform
- first contact date
- status
- interest level
- notes
- next follow-up date

## Status Labels

- New
- Contacted
- Replied
- Call booked
- Interested in lead magnet
- Interested in beta
- Not now
- Closed

## Daily Target

Start lean:
- 10 new outreach messages per weekday
- 2 follow-ups per day minimum
- 1 discovery call target every 2 to 3 days

## Success Metric

In the first 2 weeks, aim for:
- 50 to 100 outreach attempts
- 10 real replies
- 5 to 10 conversations
- 2 to 5 early buyers or strong beta prospects
