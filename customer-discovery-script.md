# Customer Discovery Script

## Goal

Learn how prospects currently find funding, where the process breaks down, and what would make a funding intelligence product worth paying for.

## Opening

Thanks for taking the time. I’m building a funding intelligence product for Canadian nonprofits and grant-focused teams, and I’m trying to understand how people actually manage funding discovery and tracking today. This is a research conversation, not a hard sell.

## Context Questions

Ask:
- What does your role involve when it comes to grants or funding?
- How often are you actively searching for opportunities?
- Are you doing this for one organization or across multiple clients or programs?

## Current Workflow Questions

Ask:
- How do you currently find grant or funding opportunities?
- What tools do you use to track them now?
- Where does the process usually become messy or time-consuming?
- How do deadlines get tracked across your team?

## Pain Questions

Ask:
- What is the most frustrating part of funding discovery right now?
- What usually gets missed?
- What causes strong opportunities to slip by?
- When this process goes badly, what does that cost your team?

## Existing Alternatives

Ask:
- Are you using spreadsheets, newsletters, consultants, internal trackers, or paid databases right now?
- What do you like about your current process?
- What do you dislike about it?
- Have you ever paid for funding intelligence or grant support before?

## Demand Questions

Ask:
- If someone gave you a clean, curated funding tracker with deadlines and fit notes, what would matter most?
- Which would be more valuable: one-time database access or an ongoing updated membership?
- What would make this immediately useful enough to pay for?
- What would stop you from paying for it?

## Pricing Questions

Ask gently:
- For an early version, would a $29 to $49 starter product feel reasonable, too low, or too high?
- For an ongoing membership with updates, does something like $39 per month sound realistic if the value is there?

## Close

Say:

This has been really helpful. Based on what I’m hearing, I’m putting together an early version of the product. If I send over the free guide or beta details when it’s ready, would you like me to include you?

## What to Capture After Each Call

- exact pain phrases they used
- current workflow summary
- current tools
- strongest frustration
- desired outcome
- pricing reaction
- objections
- whether they want early access

## Best Signals

Pay extra attention when they say:
- “We lose time searching everywhere”
- “We miss deadlines”
- “Everything is in different places”
- “I’d pay if it saved us real time”
- “We need this updated regularly”

## Red Flags

Be cautious if they say:
- “We only apply for one grant a year”
- “We already have a full internal grants team and system”
- “We would never pay for research tools”

These are useful data points, but not ideal first buyers.
