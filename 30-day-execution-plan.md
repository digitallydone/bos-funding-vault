# 30-Day Execution Plan

## Primary Objective

Validate and launch the first version of a real business around one focused offer:

Funding Intelligence Vault for Canadian nonprofits and grant-focused small organizations.

## Success Criteria for Day 30

By the end of 30 days, we should have:
- A clear niche and positioning statement
- A live lead magnet
- A simple landing page
- A starter paid offer
- A basic funding database structure
- At least 10 customer conversations
- First sales or pre-sales

## Guardrails

- Focus on one customer segment only
- Sell before overbuilding
- Use no-code and simple tools first
- Prioritize speed and proof over polish

## Week 1: Pick the Wedge

### Outcomes

- One audience
- One core promise
- One entry offer

### Tasks

1. Define the first customer:
- Canadian nonprofits
- Grant consultants
- Small mission-driven organizations

2. Write the positioning:
- We help nonprofits and grant-focused organizations find funding faster and operate with ready-to-use systems.

3. Choose the first offer name:
- Funding Intelligence Vault
- Grant Opportunity Vault
- Canadian Funding Radar

4. Decide the initial paid version:
- $49 starter database

5. Define what is inside:
- Curated opportunities
- Deadlines
- Eligibility notes
- Source links
- Basic tracker

6. List 25 places your audience already gathers:
- LinkedIn groups
- Facebook groups
- Reddit communities
- Nonprofit newsletters
- Sector-specific associations

### Deliverables

- Niche statement
- Offer name
- Offer outline
- Prospect list

## Week 2: Build the Minimum Viable Funnel

### Outcomes

- People can understand the offer
- People can join the email list
- People can buy or join a waitlist

### Tasks

1. Create the lead magnet:
- The Canadian Funding Landscape: Where to Start

2. Write the landing page:
- Headline
- Problem
- Solution
- Benefits
- CTA

3. Set up simple infrastructure:
- Carrd, Notion, or typed static page
- ConvertKit, MailerLite, or Beehiiv
- Stripe or Gumroad checkout

4. Create the first email sequence:
- Delivery email
- Story email
- Problem agitation email
- Offer email
- Last chance email

5. Build a simple CRM tracker:
- Prospect name
- Segment
- Contact date
- Response
- Call booked
- Interested in beta

### Deliverables

- Lead magnet draft
- Landing page copy
- Email sequence
- Checkout or waitlist setup

## Week 3: Customer Discovery and Beta Sales

### Outcomes

- Real conversations
- Objections collected
- Early buyers or committed testers

### Tasks

1. Reach out to 5 to 10 prospects per day

2. Use a simple message:
- I’m building a funding intelligence product for Canadian nonprofits and grant-focused teams. It helps surface relevant opportunities faster, with deadlines and research notes in one place. Would you be open to a 15-minute chat or early beta access?

3. Run 10 conversations total

4. Ask these questions:
- How do you currently find funding opportunities?
- What is the most frustrating part of the process?
- How do deadlines slip?
- What would make this worth paying for?
- Would you prefer a one-time database or an ongoing membership?

5. Offer beta pricing:
- Founding access at $29 to $49

6. Track exact phrases people use

### Deliverables

- Call notes
- Objection list
- Messaging updates
- First sales or preorders

## Week 4: Launch the First Version

### Outcomes

- Product delivered
- Buyers onboarded
- Feedback loop active

### Tasks

1. Package the first database:
- Airtable
- Notion
- Google Sheets

2. Organize fields:
- Opportunity name
- Funder
- Amount
- Deadline
- Eligibility
- Region
- Sector
- Link
- Notes

3. Add one bonus:
- Grant application checklist
- Proposal template
- Deadline planning sheet

4. Deliver to beta users

5. Ask for feedback and testimonials

6. Identify what becomes recurring:
- Weekly updates
- Monthly curated opportunities
- Sector tags
- New templates

### Deliverables

- Beta database
- Bonus asset
- Testimonial requests
- Membership roadmap

## Daily Operating Rhythm

Monday:
- Research and add new opportunities
- Improve product data quality

Tuesday:
- Outreach and calls
- Update CRM

Wednesday:
- Write content and emails
- Refine landing page copy

Thursday:
- Deliver product improvements
- Ask for feedback

Friday:
- Review metrics
- Adjust offer
- Plan next week

## Metrics to Watch

- Landing page conversion rate
- Lead magnet opt-ins
- Sales conversations booked
- Beta purchases
- Reply rate to outreach
- Most common objection

## Simple Tool Stack

Keep it lean:
- Notion or Airtable for database
- Gumroad or Stripe for payments
- ConvertKit or MailerLite for email
- Carrd or a simple static page for landing page
- Google Sheets for CRM if needed

## Founder Script

This is the operating mindset:
- Sell the smallest valuable version first
- Talk to the market every week
- Treat objections as product design input
- Focus on proof of demand before expansion

## What Comes After Day 30

If the first offer gets traction:
- Convert the database into a subscription
- Add compliance templates as cross-sells
- Add AI operating kits as upsells

If traction is weak:
- Narrow the niche further
- Adjust the promise
- Increase urgency around deadlines, compliance, or lost funding risk

## Immediate Next Actions

Do these first:
1. Confirm the first niche is Canadian nonprofits and grant-focused organizations
2. Choose the product name
3. Draft the lead magnet
4. Draft the landing page
5. Start outreach this week
