# Outreach Kickoff Pack — Week 1

Ready-to-send messages for your first week, aligned with the workspace-first positioning. Personalize the bracketed fields, swap proof points where you have them, and ship.

The mental model running through every message:

> You're not selling a spreadsheet. You're selling a working environment — a curated map of Canadian funding that lands fully configured in their tool of choice.

Where to put your effort: pick **2 LinkedIn DMs** and **1 cold email** for daily volume. Rotate them after a week so you can see which gets replies. Use the social posts to warm the audience your DMs land in — same week, same voice.

## Cadence for Week 1

Use this rhythm:

| Day | Outreach actions | Content |
|---|---|---|
| Mon | 10 cold DMs (DM 1 variant) + 5 cold emails (Email 1) | LinkedIn launch post |
| Tue | 10 cold DMs (DM 1 variant) + send guide to anyone who replied | — |
| Wed | 10 cold DMs (DM 1 variant) + Email 1 follow-ups | X / Threads post |
| Thu | DM 2 follow-ups on Mon's silent threads | — |
| Fri | DM 3 / DM 4 to anyone warm + first beta offers to qualified leads | Engage on others' posts (not self-promote) |
| Sat | Review what got replies; rewrite the message that didn't | — |
| Sun | Plan next 10 prospects and 1 new content angle | — |

Daily target: **10 cold messages + 5 follow-ups** = 15 touchpoints/day, 75 in week 1.

Realistic week 1 result with this volume:
- 8–15 replies
- 3–6 real conversations
- 1–3 founding sales

If you hit zero sales by Friday, the message isn't working — pause and rewrite. Don't push more volume through a leaky message.

## What to track

Keep a single sheet (or use Notion / Airtable) with:

- name
- role
- organization
- platform (LinkedIn / email / referral)
- date sent
- which message variant
- reply yes/no + summary
- next-action date

After week 1, the highest-leverage thing you do is **read every reply that wasn't a no**, find the language they used, and rewrite the next week's messages using their words back to them. That's how outreach gets sharper instead of louder.
