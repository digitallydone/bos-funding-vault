First 20 Minutes

The shortest path to value.

Once your tracker is imported, this four-step sequence is what turns the vault from "another spreadsheet" into a real funding map for your organization.

1. Read the public guide first

Skim the guide to understand the major source categories before you start filtering — it makes the next steps much faster.

2. Pick three rows that fit

Open three source URLs that look right for your work. Write one sentence each in notes about how they fit. That single pass changes everything.

3. Build your shortlist

Mark 10–15 sources that match your geography, issue area, and organization type. Most teams need fewer than 75 rows to track.

4. Assign a weekly owner

One person, 30 minutes a week, walking the shortlist. That cadence is what makes the vault sticky instead of forgotten.

Recommended views

  • National sources
  • Your province or region
  • Your mission area or sector
  • Rolling opportunities
  • High-priority sources for the next 90 days

First-week wins

  • Replace scattered bookmarks with one central workspace
  • Eliminate low-fit sources that waste attention
  • Identify 3 to 5 realistic opportunities to monitor closely
  • Create a calmer weekly review habit for the team
